7e:["$","p",null,{"children":"Finite sets, status tables, certificate fields, diagnostic records, audit logs, stage updates. A prototype can be built quickly."}]

80:["$","p",null,{"children":"T-ACL exposes uncomfortable things."}]
81:["$","p",null,{"children":"It may show that a legal memo did not cover the current use. It may show that a fairness conclusion depends on vendor self-certification. 8a:["$","p",null,{"children":"The first round should be retrospective coding. Take existing materials and ask how they would look under T-ACL.
